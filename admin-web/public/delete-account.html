FlowWrite — account deletion

You can permanently delete your FlowWrite account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is immediate when done in the app and cannot be undone.

Fastest way: in the app

  1. Open the FlowWrite app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

This deletes your account immediately and permanently.

Alternative: request by email

If you can't access the app, email us at flowwrite@u11.ca with the subject “Account Deletion Request” from the email address tied to your account. Email deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your profile and plan information.
  • All templates you created.
  • Your usage history and statistics.

Voice recordings are never retained in the first place — audio is deleted immediately after transcription, so there is nothing to remove.
